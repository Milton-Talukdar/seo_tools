Proportional Rasterization Mechanics

  • In Google’s standard 20px Arial typeface, a capital ‘W’ occupies 28 pixels, whereas a lowercase ‘i’ occupies only 6 pixels.
  • A 55-character title composed with wide characters or ALL-CAPS acronyms will truncate well before a 65-character title written in natural sentence casing.
  • While Google can algorithmically rewrite titles, remaining within the 600px threshold guarantees that natural snippet truncation dots (...) will not obscure your primary commercial keywords.
