Triage Protocol & Privacy Notice

  • Export two non-overlapping date ranges from Google Search Console → Performance → Export (CSV).
  • Ensure both files use the same export tab dimension (either Pages.csv or Queries.csv).
  • 100% Client-Side Processing: Your CSV performance metrics are parsed entirely in your local browser sandbox and never transmitted to external servers.
